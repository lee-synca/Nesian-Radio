The Music Behind Nesian FM

Island Reggae, Samoan classics and fresh Pasifika sounds — the heartbeat of the Pacific, playing around the clock.

Nesian FM is built around one thing: the music of the Pacific. Every hour of the day we celebrate the artists, genres and voices that carry the culture — from the roots of island reggae to the newest Pasifika releases.

Island Reggae

Island Reggae is the sound of the modern Pacific — warm, soulful and rhythmic, built on classic reggae foundations but shaped by island harmonies and storytelling. It's the music of the beach, the barbecue and the long drive home, and it sits at the very centre of what Nesian FM plays.

Samoan & Pasifika Sounds

From treasured Samoan classics to contemporary Pasifika artists, Nesian FM keeps the full spectrum of island music alive. These are the songs sung at church, at family gatherings and at every celebration — the melodies that connect generations across the ocean.

A Home for Pacific Artists

Nesian FM is proud to give Pacific Island artists a stage and a worldwide audience. By keeping island music on the air 24/7, we help both established names and rising talent reach listeners across the diaspora — from Samoa and the wider Pacific to New Zealand, Australia, the United States and beyond.
