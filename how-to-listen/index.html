How to Listen to Nesian FM

Three easy ways to tune in — all free, all 24/7, on any device.

Listening to Nesian FM is completely free and takes about ten seconds. There's no app to download and no account to create — just choose your favourite way to tune in below.

Listen in your browser (easiest)

1

Open nesianfm.com

Visit nesianfm.com in any web browser — Chrome, Safari, Edge or Firefox — on your phone, tablet, laptop or desktop.

2

Press play

Hit the play button on the live player and Nesian FM starts streaming instantly. That's it — you're listening.

3

Keep it playing

Leave the tab open and the music keeps going 24/7. Turn it up and bring the islands into the room.

Listen on TuneIn

Prefer a dedicated app? Nesian FM is on TuneIn Radio, so you can listen on the go, on smart speakers, and on your car system:

  • Open the Nesian FM page on TuneIn
  • Or search "Nesian FM" inside the TuneIn app
  • Press play and favourite the station so it's always one tap away

Listen on any device

Because Nesian FM streams over the web, it works just about everywhere:

  • Phone & tablet — iPhone, iPad and Android, in your browser or via TuneIn
  • Laptop & desktop — Windows and Mac, in any browser
  • Smart speakers & car — through the TuneIn app and compatible devices

Is it really free?

Yes — 100% free, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, with no subscription and no sign-up. Wherever you are in the world, home is only a play button away.
