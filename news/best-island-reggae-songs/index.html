Best Island Reggae Songs to Start With

Island reggae is easy to love and even easier to get lost in. If you're just discovering the warm, harmony-rich Pacific sound (new to it? start with our guide to what island reggae is), these six songs are the perfect way in. Everyone's list is different — but each of these captures the feel-good heart of the genre.

6 island reggae songs to get you started

1. J Boog — "Let's Do It Again"

Maybe the ultimate island reggae crossover anthem. Smooth, sunny and impossibly easy to sing along to — if you only press play on one song from this list, make it this one.

2. Fiji — "Sweet Darlin'"

Fiji is one of the founding voices of the island reggae sound, and "Sweet Darlin'" is a timeless, soulful classic that still fills every island party and family gathering.

3. Tomorrow People — "Souljah Feeling"

This seven-piece from Aotearoa (New Zealand) call their sound "sunshine reggae" — and "Souljah Feeling" is exactly that: warm, uplifting and impossible not to move to. The House of Shem remix turned it into a full-blown island anthem.

4. Katchafire — "Giddy Up"

Straight out of Aotearoa (New Zealand), Katchafire bring proper roots-reggae authenticity. Laid-back, groovy and effortlessly cool.

5. Three Houses Down — "Love You For Life"

A heartfelt Samoan reggae favourite and a regular on Nesian FM. Rich harmonies and pure island romance — this is the sound of home for a lot of listeners.

6. Maoli — "Rock Away"

Hawaiian island reggae with soul and heart. Gentle, warm and made for slow evenings — the kind of song you put on and never skip.

Ready for more? Artists to explore next

Once these have you hooked, dig into more of the island reggae world:

  • The Green — modern Hawaiian island-reggae flagship
  • Rebel Souljahz — smooth Hawaiian harmonies
  • Sons of Zion — chart-topping reggae from Aotearoa
  • Ekolu — classic Hawaiian island grooves
  • Anuhea — soulful, singer-songwriter island reggae
  • Bruddah Waltah — a pioneer of the Hawaiian reggae sound
