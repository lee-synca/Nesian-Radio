About Nesian FM

Free 24/7 Pacific Island radio, streaming the sound of home to our people across the world.

Nesian FM is a free, 24/7 online Pacific Island radio station broadcasting Island Reggae, Samoan and Pasifika music from Samoa (685) to the world. No subscription, no sign-up — just press play and you're home.

The Frequency of Home

For every Pacific Islander living far from the islands, music is the shortest way home. Nesian FM exists to keep that connection alive — a living thread of island reggae, Samoan classics and fresh Pasifika sounds playing around the clock, wherever you are in the world.

Whether you're in Apia, Auckland, Sydney, Brisbane, Salt Lake City or anywhere the diaspora has settled, Nesian FM brings the warmth, rhythm and pride of the Pacific straight to your speakers. That's why we call ourselves The Frequency of Home — 685 to the World.

What We Play

Our sound is unmistakably Pacific:

  • Island Reggae — the laid-back, soulful reggae that defines the modern Pacific sound
  • Samoan & Pasifika music — from beloved classics to today's artists
  • Pacific Island anthems — the songs that bring every family gathering, church and community together

The music never stops. Nesian FM streams live 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, completely free.

For the Diaspora. For the Culture. For Our People.

Nesian FM is more than a radio station — it's a gathering place. It's the soundtrack to the Sunday umu, the long drive, the homesick evening and the family celebration. We're proud to serve Pacific communities and the wider diaspora, celebrating the artists and culture that make our islands sing.
