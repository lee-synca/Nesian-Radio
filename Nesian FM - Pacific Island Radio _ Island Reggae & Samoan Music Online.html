Your Home for Pacific Island Music

Nesian FM is the online radio station built for Pacific Islanders around the world. Whether you’re in Auckland, Sydney, Los Angeles, London, or anywhere in between — we bring the sounds of the islands straight to you, 24/7.

We play the best Island Reggae and Pacific Island music from Samoan, Tongan, Fijian, Hawaiian, Cook Island, Niuean and Polynesian artists. From the roots reggae sounds of the Pacific to contemporary Pasifika music — if it comes from the islands, it belongs on Nesian FM.

This is more than a radio station. It’s a connection back home. A reminder of who we are, where we come from, and the music that raised us. Turn it on and let the islands come to you.

Pure Pacific. All Day. All Night.

Nesian FM plays the music Pacific Islanders love. Our playlist is built around:

 

Island Reggae — The sound of the Pacific. Artists like J Boog, Fiji, Katchafire, Three Houses Down, Tomorrow People, Kolohe Kai, Rebel Souljahz and the best Pacific reggae artists from New Zealand, Hawaii, Australia and across Oceania.

Pacific Island Music — Samoan music, Tongan music, Fijian music, Hawaiian music, Cook Island music and Polynesian music from artists across the Pacific. Traditional sounds and contemporary Pasifika artists side by side.

For the Diaspora. For the Culture. For Our People.

There are Pacific Islanders living in every corner of the world. In cities far from the ocean. Far from family. Far from home. Nesian FM was built for you.


We know what it feels like to miss the islands. The smell of the sea. The sound of family. The music that played at every barbecue, every church, every gathering growing up. That’s what we put on air every single day.


Nesian FM is for the Samoan community worldwide. For Tongan families abroad. For Fijian, Hawaiian, Cook Island, Niuean and Tokelauan people living overseas. For anyone who carries the Pacific in their heart no matter where they live.

The Music Behind Nesian FM

Nesian FM was built by Lee, a Pacific Islander who knows what it feels like to live far from the islands and miss the sounds of home. You might know him as DJ Damage from back in the day — now creating AI-reimagined Pacific Island classics under the name Tama Vaiusu.

After years in the Pacific Island music scene, he built Nesian FM so our people worldwide always have somewhere to tune in and feel connected to their roots, their culture, and the music that raised them.

Alongside curating the best Island Reggae and Pacific Island music from artists around the world, Tama Vaiusu also breathes new life into the beloved classics our parents and grandparents grew up with — keeping them alive for a new generation.
